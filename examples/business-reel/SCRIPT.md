# Script — Start a business

**Voice:** Aria, English (US), Microsoft neural TTS

**Delivery:** Clear, confident and conversational; brisk but with pauses between steps.

## Line 1 — START SMART (Frame 1)

**Time:** 0.00–6.23s

    Before you spend money starting a business, prove someone wants what you're selling. Here's where to start.

## Line 2 — 01 / FIND A PROBLEM (Frame 2)

**Time:** 6.23–12.27s

    One: pick a specific customer and a problem they already care about. Who are you helping?

## Line 3 — 02 / TEST DEMAND (Frame 3)

**Time:** 12.27–19.80s

    Two: talk to potential buyers. Check competitors. Test a simple version before building the whole thing.

## Line 4 — 03 / DEFINE YOUR OFFER (Frame 4)

**Time:** 19.80–25.63s

    Three: define one clear offer: what you deliver, what it costs, and why someone should choose you.

## Line 5 — 04 / CHECK THE NUMBERS (Frame 5)

**Time:** 25.63–32.43s

    Four: estimate startup costs, monthly expenses, and profit per sale. Know how you'll fund the gap.

## Line 6 — 05 / HANDLE THE SETUP (Frame 6)

**Time:** 32.43–41.30s

    Five: check local registration, tax, permit, and insurance requirements. Separate business finances and track every expense.

## Line 7 — 06 / LAUNCH SMALL (Frame 7)

**Time:** 41.30–47.27s

    Six: launch small, find your first customers, and improve from their feedback before spending more.

## Line 8 — YOUR NEXT STEP (Frame 8)

**Time:** 47.27–54.47s

    For the full step-by-step deep dive into everything we covered, head to my channel. Save this checklist.
